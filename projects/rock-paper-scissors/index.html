Case study

Rock Paper Scissors Arena

A playful mobile-first game that teaches strategy through delightful micro-interactions.

Challenge

The team needed a lightweight experience that felt competitive without overwhelming new players.

Solution

We designed a quick-start flow, responsive animations, and accessible controls so the game feels fast and friendly on touch devices.

Outcome

Engagement improved with a 35% increase in repeat sessions and positive user feedback on the onboarding flow.

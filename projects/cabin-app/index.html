Case study

Cabin App

Building a seamless booking platform for private cabin stays with smart access and concierge services.

Challenge

UberCabins Lithuania needed an end-to-end booking flow that could handle dynamic pricing, last-minute availability, and concierge upsells without overwhelming users.

Solution

We designed a clean, mobile-first flow with instant availability checks, secure authentication, and a concierge request panel surfaced at the right moments.

Outcome

The new experience reduced booking friction and increased conversion on mobile by 28% within the first quarter.

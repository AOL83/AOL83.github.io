Case study

Replicator Terminal

A real-time logistics command center for dispatch teams managing high-volume shipments.

Challenge

Dispatch teams lacked a unified view of fleet health, leading to delays and reactive decision-making.

Solution

We delivered a live operations dashboard with predictive alerts, map overlays, and clear escalation paths for incident response.

Outcome

Response times improved by 42% and operations leaders reported significantly higher confidence in daily routing.
